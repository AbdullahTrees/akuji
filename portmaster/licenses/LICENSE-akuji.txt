Akuji the Demon - origin and attribution

The game: "Akuji the Demon" (魔王のアクジ), a freeware action-platformer by
Buster (E. Hashimoto), first released in December 2001. The original official
distribution ended in 2015. The game data in assets/ is Buster's work.

The English translation: D's translation of the game (also credited as the
"Do-jin Nyuu" translation), released in 2003 and used for the English text
converted into assets/lang/en.po. The English text is D's work.

The port: the source reconstruction and the SDL2 port by abdullahtrees. The
reconstruction is a clean-room reimplementation from the original binary's
observed behaviour; no original source was available.

The port ships the original game data as a preservation measure. Permission
from the original author, or from the translation's publisher, has not been
confirmed. If you hold the rights to this work and want the data removed from
this distribution, contact the porter.

Not included: gm.dls, Microsoft's Windows GS sound bank. The game still
supports it as an optional "gm" bank; a player who owns a licensed copy may
place it in assets/soundfonts/.

The shipped font is Wine's sserife.fon, under the LGPL-2.1; its licence is in
LICENSE-wine-fonts.txt. The shipped music bank is GeneralUser GS, whose licence
is in LICENSE-GeneralUser-GS.txt.
